Our Story A Legacy of Service and Learning

The foundation stone of Arya Sr. Secondary School was laid by the late Swami Inder Vesh Ji, a learned personality and well-known social reformer. The school opened on 2 April 2001 with 300 students and one clear purpose: to bring quality education to the children of Madina and the surrounding villages.

Today, more than 1,000 students learn here across Classes [1 to 12], in Science, Commerce and Arts. The school has grown steadily, but its spirit has not changed. We serve willingly and graciously, and we believe there is greater joy in giving than in receiving.
