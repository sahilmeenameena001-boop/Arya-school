Principal's Message A Message from the Principal

Dear Parents and Students,

Every morning at Arya begins with the same feeling: the energy of active students and the dedication of talented teachers. That feeling is what I am most proud of.

When we opened our doors in 2001, we promised to give every child a strong education and strong values. Twenty-five years later, that promise guides everything we do. We want our students to dream more, learn more, do more and become more, and to grow into responsible, confident and compassionate people who make their families and society proud.

Marks matter, and we work hard for them. But character matters just as much. Honesty, respect, kindness and service to others are taught here every day, in the classroom, on the playground and on the stage.

I warmly invite you to visit us, meet our teachers and see the school for yourself.

Mr. Ashok Kumar Dangi
Principal · M.A., B.Ed. · [24 years of teaching and 18 years of administrative experience, CONFIRM current figures]
