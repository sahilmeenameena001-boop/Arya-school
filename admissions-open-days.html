Open Days Visit Us

The best way to experience Arya is to visit us in person. At our Open Days you can hear from the Principal, meet our teachers and students, walk through the classrooms, laboratories and playground, and ask about studies, activities, admissions and fees. Places must be booked in advance so that we can give every family our full attention.

What Happens on an Open Day

  1. [10:00 am]

    Welcome and registration with tea. Meet the admissions team.

  2. [10:20 am]

    Talk by the Principal on the school’s values and approach to learning.

  3. [10:50 am]

    Student-led campus tour: classrooms, library, labs, theatre, playground.

  4. [11:40 am]

    Meet the teachers and stream heads. Ask anything.

  5. [12:15 pm]

    Admissions, fees and documents explained. Enquiry desk stays open.

How to Book

Booking is free and takes a minute. Choose your Open Day, tell us how many people are coming, and we will send a confirmation by WhatsApp and SMS, with a reminder the day before.

We respect your privacy and will use your details only to respond to your booking.
