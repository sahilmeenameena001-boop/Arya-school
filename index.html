Learning Begins With Us

Arya Sr. Secondary School stands in the heart of Madina, Rohtak. Founded in 2001 with 300 students, we now teach more than 1,000 children, and our name has become one that local families trust.

We believe education should awaken curiosity, build thinking skills and shape the whole child. Rigorous academics sit alongside sport, art, music and service, so students leave us with strong results and strong character.

Our teachers know every child by name, celebrate their strengths and support them where they need it. That is how a child who arrives shy leaves as a confident young adult.
